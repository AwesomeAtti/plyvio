Explorer — candidate moves from the current position

The Section answers one question: from the position on the board, what has been played before, and how did those games end? Each row is a legal move that occurs in the selected library, carrying how often it was chosen and a bar of the results that followed it.

Rev B, 13 Sep. Layout, height, source, tooltip and interaction are all settled and drawn as decided. The rows are read-only — this release replays an existing game, and playing a move from the Explorer waits for interactive analysis. A bar is drawn the same way at every sample size — the Section states the number of games and leaves the judgement to the reader. What remains open is the colour key, the value of a label too small to draw, and reaching the game count without a mouse.
