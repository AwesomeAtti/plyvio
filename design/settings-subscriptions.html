Why the other two sections' shape does not simply copy

Engines · DatabasesSubscriptions
Is there a catalogue?Yes — a curated list to download from, which is what Available holdsNo. There are two sources, and you name a feed on one. Nothing is fetched from a list
Does an entry leave a group?Yes — Available → Installed, onceNo. A subscription is created and stays
Is status static?Effectively — ready, unless something breaksNo. It syncs, goes offline, errors, and reports new games. §3.2.3.3 already ranks these
Is there a manual action?NoYes — sync now
How many will there be?A handful to a dozenOne to four. Two sources, and most people follow one or two feeds

The count is what settles most of this. With one to four rows, any structure that adds a second group spends more chrome than it earns.

The status slot is the one genuinely new thing. Engines and Databases are static once installed; a subscription changes state on its own while the section is open. Reusing §3.2.3.3's priority is what keeps Settings and the Library Sidebar telling the same story.
